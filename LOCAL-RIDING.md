# Training with the terrain you have

Information checked 11 October 2026. The planner treats local terrain as a way to deliver a session, not a reason to increase its total minutes or guarantee identical training effects.

## Van Brienenoordbrug

Rijkswaterstaat confirms the bridge has a cycle path and publishes work/access information. Its presence makes it a relevant local crossing to inspect for short controlled efforts. This app does not claim that the path is currently open, quiet, or suitable for unrestricted hill repeats. Use the designated path, follow traffic direction and use legal return points.

The preset 60-second effort, three-minute recovery and 15-minute one-way travel time are **editable example workout settings**, not surveyed facts about the bridge. Measure your own usable segment and travel. The app changes work/recovery blocks within the original planned workout time; if travel does not fit a short session, it suggests a closer flat loop.

- [Current Rijkswaterstaat project page](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/wegen/projectenoverzicht/a16-vernieuwen-van-brienenoordbrug)
- [Rijkswaterstaat documentation of the cycle-path crossing](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2024/06/a16-met-pont-naar-overkant-tijdens-afgesloten-fietspad-van-brienenoordbrug) — the 2024 closure is historical evidence, not a current closure claim.

## Tweede Heinenoordtunnel

The tunnel complex contains separate motorway and slow-traffic facilities. The cycling/foot crossing has entrance lifts/escalators. A cycling tube is not evidence that riders may use agricultural ramps for intervals.

The preset incorporates a legal easy crossing into a local endurance ride and places sustained work on a suitable flat stretch outside the tube. It does not prescribe repeats on escalators, agricultural ramps or within a shared tunnel. If you know a distinct accessible approach you use, add it as your own named segment and mark whether you have inspected it.

- [Rijkswaterstaat tunnel overview](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/wegen/wegenoverzicht/a29/heinenoordtunnel-a29)
- [2026 slow-traffic tunnel maintenance information](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/nieuws/archief/2026/02/afsluitingen-tweede-heinenoordtunnel-voor-regulier-onderhoud-in-2026)
- [Original designer’s description of cycling entrance infrastructure](https://www.zja.nl/nl/page/982/tweede-heinenoordtunnel)

## Finding nearby candidates

The live finder geocodes a Dutch town through PDOK and searches within 5, 10 or 20 km for OpenStreetMap ways tagged as cycle-path bridges or inclines, plus bridge paths explicitly allowing bicycles. It presents up to twenty candidates, with straight-line distance from the town centre and direct map links. The response is cached in server memory and new searches are rate limited.

OSM mapping can be incomplete, stale or incorrect. A bridge may be almost flat; a tagged incline may have no numeric gradient. The finder does not calculate rideable approach distance, legal turning points, surface quality, traffic, vertical gain or opening status. Inspect a candidate before planning repetitions.

Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Town lookup: PDOK. No live GPS or training history is sent to either service.

- [PDOK Locatieserver documentation](https://github.com/PDOK/locatieserver)
- [OpenStreetMap copyright and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)

## Flat and indoor alternatives

Prescribed cycling effort can be delivered on a suitable uninterrupted flat road or trainer when hills are unavailable. This substitutes workout delivery, without claiming to reproduce climbing position, descending, steering or group-riding skill. The skills passport keeps practical preparation visible alongside fitness training.

## GPT connections

Custom GPTs in the GPT Store run in ChatGPT and cannot be directly embedded in this website. The supported in-app integration is a dedicated API coach with its own instructions and current training context. Settings accepts an OpenAI API key/model or a local Ollama model. AI plan edits remain conservative suggestions, reviewed before application. The downloadable coaching brief offers a manual way to consult another GPT in ChatGPT.
