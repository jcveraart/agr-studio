# AGR event research

Checked 11 October 2026 against organizer sources. This independent tool prepares riders for the **Toerversie**, the amateur sportive on Saturday, rather than the professional race on Sunday. No official affiliation is implied.

## Confirmed event information

- **Saturday 17 April 2027** is explicitly announced in the [official 2027 FAQ](https://www.amstel.nl/amstelgoldrace/toerversie/vraag-en-antwoord).
- The [official overview](https://www.amstel.nl/toerversie?lang=nl) lists start at Shimano Experience Center, De Leeuwhof 2, Valkenburg; finish in Valkenburg aan de Geul; six distance options; support services; and registration from 1–31 October 2026.
- The [lottery FAQ](https://www.amstel.nl/vragen-over-de-loting) announces week 45 of 2026 for the first draw, week 48 for a second draw, and an exchange opening in early February 2027 for eligible applicants who registered during October. Registration for the draw is free; participation requires subsequent payment.
- The [registration page](https://www.amstel.nl/amstelgoldrace/inschrijven-2026), despite its legacy URL, refers to 2027 and links to the [2027 Atleta registration](https://atleta.cc/e/ulVqtS9Baj1g). The registration-period FAQ also contains stale "not possible" text. The app therefore shows the published registration window and directs users to the organizer rather than asserting real-time ticket availability.

## Practical information and caution about older pages

The [participation FAQ](https://www.amstel.nl/vragen-over-jouw-deelname) gives these suggested start windows: 65 km 08:30–11:00, 100 km 08:00–11:00, 125 km 07:30–11:00, 150 km 07:00–11:00, 200/240 km 06:30–09:00. It lists collection on Friday 16 April 12:00–21:00 and Saturday 17 April 06:00–10:30, requiring ID plus the collection email. Feed-stop locations follow in the final participant email. Riders should reconfirm practical arrangements before travelling.

The [participant terms](https://www.amstel.nl/amstelgoldrace/belangrijke-informatie), currently version 1 October 2024, require age 18+, visible rider identification, a helmet and a roadworthy bicycle. They classify the event as a touring sportive and require normal road rules. Follow the terms presented during registration if updated.

Do not source current price or parking from the unversioned [legacy routes page](https://www.amstel.nl/routes): it contains an old event date and contradictory details. The cost FAQ explicitly labels its price as **2026**, so the app intentionally does not display it as a confirmed 2027 price. Route options and hill demands are suitable inputs for training goals; exact race-day arrangements remain organizer-controlled.

## JOIN product inspiration

The [official JOIN onboarding guide](https://help.join.cc/hc/en-150/articles/4402679265809-Setting-up-your-first-JOIN-training-plan) describes plans based on cycling level, goal and availability, with event date/distance/ascent inputs and activity feedback. [JOIN availability documentation](https://help.join.cc/hc/en-150/articles/4404769608721-Availability) describes a default week plus individual-day overrides. [JOIN activity feedback](https://join.cc/cycling-tips/feedback-in-seconds.-a-smarter-plan-right-away.) compares planned versus completed activities and uses RPE. These are useful interaction patterns: goal setup, a clear daily workout, a flexible weekly calendar and a brief post-workout feedback flow. They provide product inspiration, not evidence validating this app's own training engine. No JOIN branding, proprietary plan or imagery is copied.
